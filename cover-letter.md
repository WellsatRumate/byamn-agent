I'm a builder. In 31 days, I took Rumate from concept to a production-grade agentic AI platform serving real tenants in Toronto - streaming LLM pipeline, RAG retrieval over 844 legal knowledge chunks, an agentic task queue with human-in-the-loop approval, and a dual-LLM deliberation system for review classification. I'm writing because the AI Builder role describes exactly how I work: end-to-end ownership, rapid prototyping, and shipping real systems that people actually use.

Your job description asks for someone who thinks in workflows, not just models. At Rumate, every system I've built reflects that. Our notification pipeline moves through eight orchestrated jobs: inbound email ingestion via Resend, OCR extraction, intent classification by a Sonnet-powered Inbox Agent (with Haiku for labelling), and structured routing - each step designed around human-AI handoffs and decision boundaries. Our T6 tribunal filing flow guides tenants through six steps with auto-validation, PDF generation, and guided checklists - treating regulatory compliance and user trust as core design constraints, not afterthoughts. I built the agentic pipeline (Tori drafts, action queue, n8n webhook, admin approval) in a single day. These aren't demos. They're in production.

On the technical side, I work across the stack that enterprise AI demands: Next.js and Supabase with RLS policies, pgvector for semantic search, prompt caching across all call sites, Claude and Qwen models orchestrated for different judgment layers, and Edge Functions for daily batch operations. I've built reusable components throughout - a verification sub-agent with privacy-by-design (OCR extracts five fields, stores only an attestation against a hashed user ID, deletes the source document), a scraping framework with clear escalation rules (fetch first, headless browser only when needed), and modular scoring pipelines that combine municipal, tenant-reported, and public sentiment data into a unified intelligence layer. I understand what it takes to build things that persist in regulated, complex environments.

Before Rumate, I founded and scaled GetCAPPD, a custom manufacturing business serving enterprise clients including SharkNinja, Paramount, and Soho House - so I understand how large organizations operate, how decisions get made across stakeholders, and what "production" means when reputation is on the line. I closed that business profitably and redirected everything into this. I'm comfortable operating in ambiguity because that's been the entire story: pivoting from a two-sided marketplace to a tenant-first B2C2G model mid-build, navigating Law Society regulations to ship a compliant tribunal filing product, and shipping features against real user feedback from beta tenants documenting active building violations.

What draws me to this role specifically is the AI Lab environment and the shared builder pool model. I've been building alone or with a small co-founder team - the chance to contribute reusable assets, run experiments with frontier models inside secure infrastructure, and work alongside builders, researchers, and designers on high-leverage internal problems is compelling. I want to be in a room where the work compounds.

I'd welcome the chance to talk through any of this in more detail - or better yet, to show you what I've built.
