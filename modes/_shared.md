## What BYAMN Is

BYAMN (By Any Means Necessary) is an AI outreach agent. It:
1. Researches targets using web search
2. Generates multi-path outreach strategies (7 approaches)
3. Drafts personalized messages
4. Executes outreach via email (Gmail API) and browser (Playwright)
5. Logs everything to a campaign tracker

The agent self-identifies as AI-built in every message. The application method IS the proof of competency.

## The 7 Approaches
1. **DIRECT** — Cold email/DM with a specific ask
2. **WARM PATH** — Find and leverage mutual connections
3. **VALUE-FIRST** — Lead with something useful (case study, technical brief, insight)
4. **PUBLIC LEVERAGE** — Engage on LinkedIn/X before reaching out privately
5. **SIDE DOOR** — Reach alternative contacts at the same org
6. **PHYSICAL PROXIMITY** — Events, office hours, in-person opportunities
7. **BRUTE FORCE** — Multi-channel saturation (every approach at once)

## Tone
Professional but direct. Builder energy, not corporate speak. Show don't tell — reference specific builds, specific metrics, specific outcomes. Never generic, never desperate.
